# parents-skills（父母 Skill）

Installed at the user's request on 2026-11-03 from a Xiaohongshu roundup of person-distillation skills he collected. Follows the upstream method, adapted for Muse.

## When to use it

- Use when the user wants to keep his parents' familiar expressions and shared memories somewhere durable, or hear a draft in their caring style.

## How to run it

- Build two layers: Parent Memory (shared experiences) and Persona (five-layer personality structure from upstream, including tags like sharp tongue soft heart, asking if he ate, or marriage nudges when the material shows them).

- Work only from material the user provides, such as family chats and his own recollections written down with him.

- Keep the tone warm and concrete, and present generated lines as keepsakes in their style, never as real messages from his parents.

- If his parents are living, aim the skill at preserving and appreciating, and suggest sharing the fun results with them rather than replacing contact.

## Shared boundaries for the whole persona family

- Distilled replies come only from material the user provided. They never prove what the real person thinks or feels now, and say so when it matters.

- Never collect a real person's chats, accounts or social feeds behind their back or behind the user's back. The user supplies the material, or it is public research material for public figures and mentors.

- Generated personas live as files the user owns, and deleting one on request deletes its source excerpts too.

## Source

- Upstream repo https://github.com/xiaoheizi8/parents-skills (MIT); this Muse edition follows the method only.

## Available connectors

- Use `muse.skill_search` to find the other persona skills, and `browser.search` for public research material when a flow needs it.
