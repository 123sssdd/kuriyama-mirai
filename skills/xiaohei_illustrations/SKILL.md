## When to use it

- Use when an article or explanation needs a memorable visual: one judgment, flow or metaphor per illustration, drawn around the little black figure Xiaohei.

## How to run it

- The skill itself produces the image prompts and storyboard; an image backend does the actual drawing. In Muse, generate with Muse's own media tools in the same hand-drawn style instead of stopping at prompts.

- One idea per picture, 16:9, with Xiaohei acting the metaphor out. If the figure is decoration instead of explanation, redo the storyboard.

- Match illustrations to specific paragraphs he names or that carry the article's key judgments, and show him the storyboard before generating the set.
