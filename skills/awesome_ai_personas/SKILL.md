# awesome-ai-personas（人物技能合集）

Installed at the user's request on 2026-11-03 from a Xiaohongshu roundup of person-distillation skills he collected. Follows the upstream method, adapted for Muse.

## When to use it

- Use this as the map when the user wants to distill a kind of person and is not sure which installed skill fits, or wants to discover more persona skills.

## How to run it

- Route by relationship: colleague to distilly, boss to boss_skills, ex to ex_skill, crush to crush_skills, self to yourself_skill, parents to parents_skills, mentor to supervisor_skill, senior to senpai_skill.

- Compare candidates on input material needed, layers built, and delete or rollback support before recommending one.

- For new finds beyond the installed eight, check the upstream collection https://github.com/mailbus/awesome-ai-personas (CC0) and then verify the candidate repo itself before installing.

- Carry the shared warning into every recommendation: skills speak from provided material, sounding like someone is not knowing what they think.

## Shared boundaries for the whole persona family

- Distilled replies come only from material the user provided. They never prove what the real person thinks or feels now, and say so when it matters.

- Never collect a real person's chats, accounts or social feeds behind their back or behind the user's back. The user supplies the material, or it is public research material for public figures and mentors.

- Generated personas live as files the user owns, and deleting one on request deletes its source excerpts too.

## Source

- This is a navigation skill for the persona family installed on 2026-11-03; keep it updated when the family grows or merges.

## Available connectors

- Use `muse.skill_search` to find the other persona skills, and `browser.search` for public research material when a flow needs it.
